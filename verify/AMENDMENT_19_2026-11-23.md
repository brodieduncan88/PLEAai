## What it does (D20-F)

A JSON number a check prints is read as the decimal its digits write (`parse_float=decimal.Decimal`) and compared exactly, so a
value on the wrong side of a limit by less than a binary float resolves is no longer signed PASS. The signed-record contract is
unchanged (Option A): where the record's 28-digit form would write a value otherwise than as decided, the record's checker
refuses the verdict and the run is UNKNOWN: CHECK_OUTPUT_INVALID, an existing reason. No tolerance, no epsilon, and no decimal
is made into a float. Option B (exact tokens through the whole record) would change the signed bytes or the numerical
contract, and was not implemented: it is the owner's to approve. `verify/AMEND19_DEV_LOG.md` entries 1 to 4 give the
counterexample, the trace of where precision goes, the two options, and the correction. `verify/DECLARED_AMEND19.md` gives
the changed files with their hashes and the expectations.
