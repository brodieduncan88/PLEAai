== HIGH ==

**N-H1. The console names the record's row from a `jobs.json` entry it does not match to the job. A folder writer can get a revoked job admitted again while the console exits 0.** RUN: `q1_console_substitution.out`.
- **Where.**
  - front_ctl.py:164-170: `_current` checks only that the entry is signed by the engineer, not that `b['job'] == job`.
  - front_ctl.py:250: a new REVOKE takes its `cert_sha256` from that entry.
  - front_ctl.py:241-249: if the entry is already a REVOKE, it is recorded again and returned as success, whatever job it names.
  - front_ctl.py:222: rebind has the same flaw.
- **Q1a (a new revoke).**
  - The adversary sets `jobs['job-x']` to job-y's ENROL. The engineer runs `revoke job-x`, and it returns normally.
  - The REVOKE it signs says job-x but carries job-y's certificate hash. It is recorded under the row named by job-y's certificate hash and "job-x", which no binding ever uses.
  - job-x's real row stays (1, False). The adversary puts job-x's ENROL back, and the front door admits job-x: `q1a_admitted_after_put_back: true`.
  - The cost to the adversary: job-x is refused while the substitution is in place. After the revocation the job stays usable indefinitely.
- **Q1b (the M1 path, through the CLI).**
  - The adversary sets `jobs['job-w']` to any earlier REVOKE this engineer signed (here job-z's). `front_ctl.py revoke HOME job-w` exits 0 with nothing on stderr.
  - Nothing is recorded or written for job-w. The only sign is `"job": "job-z"` in the JSON printed on stdout.
  - The adversary puts job-w's ENROL back, and job-w is admitted: `q1b_admitted_after_put_back: true`.
- **Control (Q1c).** With an honest file, the same steps end refused.
- **Regression.** At 3473478, the same substitution as Q1a recorded under the realm and the job argument, and the put-back was refused (RUN: `q6_q1a_at_3473478.out`, `q6a_admitted_after_put_back: false`).
- **Inaccurate claims this contradicts.**
  - front_state.py:38-40 says "a binding with another certificate's hash is not one the engineer signed". It is: it was signed for another job.
  - README_FRONT.md:183-185 says a revoked job is refused for good.
  - The uncommitted register text says "No realm, file or setting bears on it".
  - `front_ctl.py jobs` and the console's JSON never show `cert_sha256`, so the engineer cannot see which enrolment was acted on.
- **Fix options (the author's decision).**
  - At minimum, refuse in `_current` when `b['job'] != job`, which also covers the re-record branch. That closes Q1a and Q1b, but not N-M1.
  - To close N-M1 as well: record each enrolment in the database at enrol time, and have enrol refuse a name the database already holds, so "a job name is used once" is enforced there, not by the file. Or record every revocation also under a row named by the job alone, and have the front door refuse on either row.

== THE QUESTIONS ==

**Q1.** Answered in the table above.

**Q2: can anyone short of the engineer's key get a revoked or superseded binding admitted?**
- **A folder writer:** yes, by N-H1 (RUN, console exit 0) and N-M1 (RUN, needs a re-enrolment). Also by L1, the stated limit. The same substitution through rebind would record a move under the wrong row, leaving the old binding open to a put-back. ARGUED, and only when both jobs share a baseline, since otherwise rebind refuses.
- **CAP-DB alone:** no (RUN, Q2d).
  - Calling the function with "not revoked" and a higher number on a revoked row answers REVOKED, and the row stays revoked.
  - A made-up high number makes the real binding STALE: a denial, not an admission.
  - Reading the table directly is refused (InsufficientPrivilege).
  - The function's search_path is pg_catalog, pg_temp, and the table name is qualified.
- **The assistant's side with a job certificate only:** no (ARGUED).
  - The name comes from the certificate's CN (tls.py:178), so a caller with another job's certificate reaches only its own job's row.
  - The admit order, the record step before the certificate check, only moves the row to the file's signed binding. In the in-process worst case (Q2b, RUN), a move advanced the row to (2, False), a put-back answered STALE and left it unchanged, and nothing was admitted.
- **Two front doors sharing a database:** rows are separate per enrolment (RUN, Q2e).
  - A revocation at one front door does not touch the other.
  - The first front door's revoked binding put into the second's file is refused.
  - An unrevoked binding of the second front door's put into the first's file is admitted in process. Over TLS that needs a shared authority. It was also admitted at 3473478, and it is outside D16.
- **Length bound:** the row name is at most 64 + 1 + 41 characters, since JOB_NAME allows 41, against a limit of 200. Fine (ARGUED).
