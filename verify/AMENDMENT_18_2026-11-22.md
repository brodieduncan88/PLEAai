is not 64 lower-case hex digits, it is not made in process and does not start, before anything is opened, written or
bound. The configuration is the existing repeated `--engineer-key` option. Rotation of the key is not supported in this
assessment profile, and the readme gives the steps of a new start. `verify/DECLARED_AMEND18.md` gives the changed files,
with their hashes, and the expectations.
