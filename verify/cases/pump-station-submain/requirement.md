# REQ-EL-021 rev A: Pump station sub-main, voltage drop and coordination

Source: site electrical design basis EDB-07, section 3.

## Requirement

The 400 V sub-main to the Eastern pump station shall keep the total voltage drop from the point of supply to the
pump-station terminals within 5 % of 400 V at the design current, and the breaker and cable shall be coordinated.

## Acceptance

1. Total voltage drop, point of supply to pump-station terminals: at most 5.0 % of 400 V. The supply is from the
   distributor's shared low-voltage network, so the 5 % limit applies.
2. Coordination: Ib <= In <= Iz, with Iz corrected for site ambient temperature.

## Design basis (fixed)

- 400 V three-phase, 50 Hz, balanced.
- Design current Ib = 120 A per phase: assessed maximum demand 100 A plus the site's 20 % spare allowance.
- Power factor 0.85 lagging.
- Installed route length 400 m (surveyed, including risers and tails).
- 1.2 % of voltage drop is already used elsewhere between the point of supply and the load (consumer mains 0.9 %,
  final connections 0.3 %).
- Site ambient: 50 C shaded air, 35 C soil at 0.5 m.
- Cable: 4-core plus earth copper, XLPE insulated, 0.6/1 kV. One cable per phase.

## What may be varied

- Conductor size: 25, 35, 50, 70 or 95 mm2. 95 mm2 is the largest conductor the existing switchboard terminals and
  the pump-station incomer accept.
- Installation method: on cable ladder in air, direct buried, or in underground duct.
- Breaker trip rating: 100, 125, 160, 200 or 250 A (the existing 250 A frame).

Changes to this requirement or to the design basis need the responsible engineer's approval (K. Dalton).
