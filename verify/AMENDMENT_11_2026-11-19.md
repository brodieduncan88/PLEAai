# Amendment 11, 19 November 2026

Private working material. Times are UTC.

## What it repairs

A notice to the responsible engineer was a file the service wrote about itself, into an inbox it controls, and nothing
showed that one had reached the engineer. This is D12 in the defect register and release gate G5, "independent notification
path with delivery evidence". `DECLARED_AMEND11.md` states the changes, the expected outcomes, the development work and what
stays open.

In short:

- **A courier reads only the witness.** It has its own key and certificate, and no credential of the service's. It reads
  the decisions since it last looked, against a checkpoint it asks for with a nonce of its own, and each run's gate reports
  and stored result, believed only if the gate signed them. For each run that failed, did not decide, was interrupted, was
  made indeterminate by the control plane, or has had no outcome for 15 minutes, it signs a notice in plain words.
- **The engineer's mailbox signs a receipt.** The courier delivers each notice to a mailbox the engineer runs, with its own
  key and folder. The mailbox keeps the notice whole and answers with a signed receipt naming it. A notice is delivered only
  on such a receipt; one not delivered waits for the next pass.
- **The courier says when it has stopped.** If the witness contradicts what the courier read before, gives what it cannot
  read, or cannot be reached, the courier tells the mailbox, in a signed notice.
- **A record can carry the notices, and its checker reads them.** The checker's tenth check verifies each notice and
  receipt against the record, words included, and the checker says which runs that call for a notice have one delivered and
  which have none. `--require-delivery` makes that a condition of its exit status.
- **The witness gives the courier its chain**, through two readings that change nothing, open only to a name admitted to
  them (`--tls-allow-chain`). The keeper of Amendment 10 still reads checkpoints only.

The service's own notices are unchanged: the courier is a second path beside them.

## What changed on the way

The code went through four forms before the declaration. A first trial showed that the governor records an outcome with
the witness a moment before it stores the result, so the courier waits before it calls a run's result unverified.

Three independent reviews were made, each of the fixes for the one before.

- The first reported two findings of high weight. A run made indeterminate by the control plane was never noticed. The
  courier would have stopped for good on a budget of a few hundred runs, because one page of decisions grew past its limit.
  It also reported five of medium weight:
  - one idle connection held up the mailbox;
  - answers the courier could not read crashed it;
  - a courier that stopped told no one;
  - a name allowed to read checkpoints could read every decision;
  - optional notices made a record unverifiable.
- The second reported one of high weight: a run the witness would not answer for hid every run listed after it. It also
  reported four of medium weight.
- The third, asked for findings of medium weight or more, reported four, none of high weight:
  - a run read only every other time never got its notice;
  - a transport fault stopped the courier for good;
  - a hung witness slowed every pass;
  - two of R26's claims were not exercised.

Each was fixed, or is stated as open, and the declaration lists them one by one. R26 grew from 18 checks to 27. The fourth
form, which the declaration carries, was not itself read by a reviewer.

A pass of 55 deliberate defects on the fourth form caught 51. Three of the four missed showed checks that passed because
another rule caught the same case first. Those checks were made to test one thing each, and the three were then caught.
The fourth missed defect cannot be seen, because another check covers the same case, and it was taken off the list. All 54
kept are caught, and the control passes.

## What stays open

The declaration's "What stays open" section is the full list. The main items are:

- A receipt says the engineer's mailbox holds a notice, not that the engineer read it.
- The courier believes the witness. It catches a witness that contradicts what it read before, not one that tells it one
  consistent false chain. The witness reads the same database as the service, and one certificate authority, run by the same
  party, issues every certificate.
- In the demonstration, the courier, the mailbox, the witness and the service share one machine and one account.
- A courier that is not running tells no one: there is no heartbeat.
- A record counts only runs with a final outcome as calling for a notice; a run with no outcome yet is noticed by the
  courier but not required by the record.
