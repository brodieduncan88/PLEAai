# Amendment 5: no line of a notice reads as saying what is not so

13 November 2026. Private working material. Internal evidence: written, run and checked by the same party.

This amends `DECLARED_AMEND4.md` and `AMENDMENT_4_2026-11-12.md`, and the declarations before them. None of them is
changed. `DECLARED_AMEND5.md`, committed before the runs reported here, says what was changed, what each test was
expected to show and what was done in development.

## Summary

1. **The last of the eighth defect is repaired.** After Amendment 4 the requirement line of every notice still said
   "Baseline …, unchanged.", true of the signed card, but standing under the first line of a changed check, where a reader
   could take it for the check. The word is gone; the line ends at the baseline's hash. The owner asked for this on
   13 November.
2. **More lines of the same kind are repaired.** The Check line now says "Baselined hash". When the gate holds no
   usable card, the answer says the card is the copy "read for this answer", which is so on the front door's route as
   well as the first client's. The front door's own text, used when it cannot read the card, names the gate's reason
   and says the gate read the card when the gate decided with it, and sets the design out as sent, in quotes. The count
   of a sender's names not shown no longer calls them "not the baseline's" when no baseline is held to tell. And a
   pass says what a failure on matching files says of the card and of the check's files.
3. **The tests, on the one official run of each file:** first plug-in 18 of 18 tests, 99 of 99 checks; front door
   21 of 21 tests, 110 of 110 checks.
4. **The review found weak tests, four times.** The restated tests at first guarded the word and not the line: 20 of
   a reviewing agent's 28 wrong versions passed, among them ", not changed." on the notice being repaired. Held then to
   exact forms for two lines, they let 12 of a re-checking agent's 14 through, each a claim on another line. Held to
   the rows `face()` writes, they let 18 of a further agent's through: a second Reason row, a criterion not met shown "met",
   a claim made to look like a sender's line, and others; and that agent found one untrue line in the repair itself, a
   count of names. Held to their rows in order and number, they let ten of a last agent's slips through: a
   refusal's Reason row reading "Every baselined limit was met", a percentage off by a factor of 100, and others.
   R18 now also holds each criterion row to agree with itself and the Reason row of a failure to the rows not met, and
   F21 the front door's own text line for line for every reason. Against the tests as declared, 86 of 86
   planted defects were caught: 17 of mine and 69 of four agents'. Earlier passes on the first, second and third
   forms caught 8 of 8, 12 of 12 and 57 of 57. A fourth pass, on the fifth form as first
   written, caught all of them too, but one stopped R18 with an error; two lines of R18 were changed so that it fails
   by name, and the pass was made again.
5. **No verdict, record or status was ever wrong because of this.**
6. **What this does not show.** Nobody outside has reviewed it; the reviews were made by AI agents working for the
   party that wrote the code. Everything ran on one machine. Whether an engineer reading a notice is in fact not misled
   is not tested.

## Finding 8, continued

**What was wrong.** In the notice of an UNKNOWN given because a data file was changed, Amendment 4 left these lines:

```
UNKNOWN: THE CHECK OR ITS DATA NO LONGER MATCHES THE BASELINE
...
              baselined ... by K. Dalton, responsible engineer.  Baseline 832eaca1ec446a74…, unchanged.
...
Check         CHK-EL-VD v..., written by ..., reviewed by ....  Hash ...…
```

"Unchanged" meant the signed card, and "Hash" the baselined one, but each stood where a reader could take it for the
check that had just been found changed.

**The repair.** The requirement line ends at the hash ("Baseline 832eaca1ec446a74…"), or with ", as read for this answer."
when no usable card is held; the Check line says "Baselined hash"; a pass says "The requirement, the limits and the load
case are as baselined." and "The check and its data matched their baselined hashes when it ran."; the front door's own
text, used when it cannot read the card, begins with the gate's reason when the gate decided with its check, ends by
saying the gate read the card as signed when it did, and sets the design out as the gate's notice does, under "the
front door holds no usable baseline to set it against"; and the count of a sender's names not shown no longer calls them
"not the baseline's" when no baseline is held to tell.

**Shown by** R18, restated (through the first client, and every reason on two cards with five designs in process; every
line held to the rows `face()` writes, in order and number), and F21, restated (through the front door, and the front
door's own text in process, line for line, for every reason).
