## What it does

It takes four findings of an external AI review of `b97fe6b` (a different system's, read-only; its report is kept separately,
and the author verified F-1 and F-2 in process before acting):
- **F-1.** A card's bounds (plausible range, design-space range) were read with `Decimal(float)` when written as numbers, so a
  bound could widen or shift: 0.29999999999999999 passed a plausible minimum of 0.3, 99999999999999995000000 sat inside a space
  from 1e23, and the exact edges 0.1 and 0.3 were refused. Bounds are now read with `dec()`, and the card rule requires them to be
  written as text.
- **F-2.** A released design file given to `check_record.py --design` was read as plain JSON (2.5000000000000001 hashed as 2.5;
  a repeated key kept the last). It is now read strictly, and the front door and the command line refuse the same numbers and
  keys.
- **F-4, the part that is not a policy.** `-0` is never written; a reason quotes a non-number only in bounded form.
  `MAX_PLAIN` was deferred by the owner.
- **F-9.** Check 6 compares the verdict's design with the one sent by `canonical()`, so 3, 3.0 and True are three designs.

`verify/DECLARED_AMEND20.md` gives the files, their hashes, and the expectations; `verify/AMEND20_DEV_LOG.md` entries 1 to 5 the
development.
