## What it does

Four of the hardening plan's Stage A items, as `verify/DECLARED_AMEND17.md` states them:

- **A1: D5 and D21.**
  - The gate host refuses a baseline that does not match the budget an attempt was admitted under, before anything
    is claimed or signed.
  - Attempt identities and request keys use the whole SHA-256.
  - A budget made before keeps its earlier 64-bit form, and a budget of both forms makes no further attempt.
- **A2: D16.** The latest binding of each job, and its revocation, are kept in PostgreSQL behind one SECURITY DEFINER
  function.
  - Revocation is absorbing, and a stale or conflicting binding admits nobody.
  - A job is named there by the engineer's key and the job's name.
  - The engineer's console records each move and revocation, and the front door records every revocation it reads,
    at its start and on its look.
- **A3: D20.** A baseline's controls are put to the record checker's own rules before the baseline is made.
- **A4: D6 and D35.**
  - The first client's transport is retired.
  - R1 to R13 run through the real front door, with every assertion and count of checks they had kept.
  - F1 and F2 are retired, their properties carried by R30 and F7.
  - The readmes are corrected.

`plea_gateway/` (the gate host's binder and the governor's identity) and `mcp/` (the key's form, and the tool list) are
