# Amendment 4: a notice says only what is so of its run

12 November 2026. Private working material. Internal evidence: written, run and checked by the same party.

This amends `DECLARED_VERIFY.md`, `DECLARED_FRONT.md`, `DECLARED_AMEND2.md`, `DECLARED_AMEND3.md` and the amendments that
go with them. None of them is changed. `DECLARED_AMEND4.md`, committed before the runs reported here, says what was
changed, what each test was expected to show and what was done in development.

## Summary

1. **The record's eighth defect is repaired.** The notice of a FAIL or an UNKNOWN said "Nothing was changed.  The
   requirement, the limits, the load case and the check are exactly as baselined." whatever the reason, so the notice
   of an UNKNOWN given because the check or its data was changed said the opposite of its reason. Each line of the
   notice now says only what is so for its verdict. The owner asked for this on 12 November.
2. **Two limits of the same kind are repaired with it.** The Verdict row now states the verdict. A revised baseline's
   banner now gives how many runs on the old baseline were met, not met and unknown, not only how many were not met.
3. **The tests, on the one official run of each file:** first plug-in 18 of 18 tests, 99 of 99 checks; front door
   21 of 21 tests, 109 of 109 checks.
4. **The review found more of the same, and weak tests.** Two AI agents found, separately, that when the gate holds no
   usable copy of the card the first client's answer still named the wrong cause; that is repaired too, and R14's check
   of that answer's first line, which held it to the wrong words, is restated. A third agent showed that the new tests
   would have passed 19 of its 32 wrong versions of the repair. The tests were tightened before the declaration.
   Against the tests as declared, 43 of 43 planted defects were caught: 24 of mine and the 19 of the agent's that had
   passed. Two earlier passes caught 16 of 16 and, stopped part-way, 7 of 7.
5. **No verdict, record or status was ever wrong because of this.** What could mislead was what a notice, an answer or
   a banner told a person who read it.
6. **What this does not show.** Nobody outside has reviewed it; the reviews were made by AI agents working for the
   party that wrote the code. Everything ran on one machine. Whether an engineer reading a notice is in fact not misled
   is not tested.

## Finding 8. A notice that says what is not so of its run

**What was wrong.** The text `face()` writes is both the engineer's notice and the answer to the caller. For every FAIL
and UNKNOWN it said:

- "Nothing was changed.  The requirement, the limits, the load case and the check are exactly as baselined." For an
  UNKNOWN given because the check or its data no longer matched the baseline, the reason two lines above said the
  opposite.
- "This result says what the declared check returned against the baselined limits." when no check had run, or none
  of its numbers had been used.
- A next step of "a different design inside the baselined design space" when no design can be verified until the
  check's files are restored.
- "Only ... can accept a failure" when the result was UNKNOWN.

And when the gate holds no usable copy of the card, the first client's answer began "UNKNOWN: THE CHECK OR ITS DATA NO
LONGER MATCHES THE BASELINE", called the baseline unchanged, and named the check's files as what to restore. The card
was what could not be used. The engineer's notice for that run, the tool's own text, was right.

**What it could not do.** The first line of every notice gave the verdict, and the verdict, the record, its checks and
its status were as they should be. No pass could be made or hidden by it.

**How it was found.** By an AI agent reviewing revision 8 of the white paper, on 12 November; the white paper has listed
it since as the eighth defect, open. It is as old as the plug-in. The no-card answer was found by two AI agents
reviewing this repair.

**The repair.** Each line now says only what is so for its verdict:

- "The requirement, the limits and the load case are as baselined.", because the card is checked before it is used.
- What is known of the check's files, on a line of its own, only where `evaluate()` alone gives the reason: that they
  matched their hashes (LIMIT_NOT_MET, RESULT_IMPLAUSIBLE), or that they did not, so the check was not run
  (BASELINE_MISMATCH). Otherwise nothing is said of them.
- "Only ... can accept this result or issue a revised baseline."
- For a changed check: restore the check and its data to the files whose hashes the baseline holds, or issue a revised
  baseline; until then no design inside the design space can be verified against it. No other design is offered.
- The closing line says what the check returned only when its numbers were compared with the limits.
- When the gate holds no usable card: "UNKNOWN: THE VERIFICATION SERVICE HOLDS NO USABLE COPY OF THIS BASELINE", the
  baseline "as this client holds it", that nothing was checked against it, and that the stored baseline is to be
  restored.

**Shown by** R18 (through the first client, with every reason the gate gives, and every reason on two cards in process)
and F21 (through the front door). R14 shows the no-card answer's first line for fourteen unusable stored baselines.

## Two limits repaired with it

**The Verdict row.** It said "Computed by the verification service from the check's output and the baselined limits."
for every verdict and did not say what the verdict was. It now begins with it: "Verdict       FAIL." Then, for a verdict
reached by comparing the check's numbers with the limits, that sentence; for any other, "Decided by the verification
service, not by comparing the check's numbers with the limits." `DECLARED_AMEND3.md` said the verdict was on the
notice's first line "and in its closing lines"; it was not in the closing lines.

**The banner of a revised baseline.** It gave the runs on the baseline it replaced and how many did not meet the
requirement. In the demonstration that read "after 6 official runs on it, of which 3 did not meet the requirement";
the other three were unknown, and a reader of the new record alone could take them for passes. The record's print now
says "after N official runs on it: K met, M not met, U unknown."; the requirement shown by the front door and by the
first client says "(N official runs, M not met); U unknown, K met." The order differs because F11, as declared, reads
the second line's first words. The counts were always in the signed card.

## Putting the repair into use

- The gate host loads the tool when it starts, so it has to be started again, on the same state file. The front door
  and the first client read the changed modules when they start.
- A notice already in an inbox is a file and is not rewritten. Verdicts and records are not changed: none of this is in
  what is signed.
- The sample transcripts in `verify/sample/` and the results of earlier official runs show the wording as it was.

  repaired here; the closing lines did not state the verdict.

  Its one line is restated in `DECLARED_AMEND4.md`.
