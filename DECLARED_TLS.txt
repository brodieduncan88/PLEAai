amendment 1, 2026-10-12T10:44:22Z, after the declared chain finished. T1-T4 35/35 and every re-run passed except W4, which failed on its first crash point before any check: the client raised TransportError where W4 expects WitnessUnavailable. Cause: is_transport_error (tls.py) counted a connection closed without a response (RemoteDisconnected, ConnectionResetError) as a transport refusal, so a service that died mid-acceptance was reported as "retrying will not help". That classification is wrong: the client cannot tell a crash from plain HTTP spoken to a TLS port, and a crash must be retried (contract 5.2/2, 5.5/2). Fix: only a TLS failure (ssl.SSLError, directly or as a URLError's reason) is a TransportError; a closed connection is WitnessUnavailable at the witness client and the ConnectionError it always was at the host client. T1's two plain-HTTP checks now expect those; the other 33 checks are unchanged. The declared W4 output is kept in outputs/declared_w4/. Re-run: t_tests.py and w4_service_crash.py; nothing else exercises the changed line.
